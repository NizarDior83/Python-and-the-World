## GENERATION 1 — Logo Mark

> Goal: The WorldBites icon mark, clean and isolated, ready to be placed over any background.

---

**CHARACTER:**
A stylized 3D clay toy globe icon — the kind you would find in a premium children's toy shop. The sphere has a warm radial gradient from bright golden yellow at the upper-left highlight fading to rich deep amber at the lower right, giving it a satisfying clay depth. The globe's surface has simplified organic continent-shaped blobs in soft teal-mint — abstract, not geographically accurate, just three or four friendly blob shapes suggesting land scattered across the sphere. A fork and a spoon cross at the center of the globe like an X, rendered in dark chocolate brown, with thick rounded strokes, blunt rounded ends, no sharp corners anywhere. The fork is on the left, three rounded tines; the spoon is on the right, small oval bowl. The overall object feels tactile, squeezable, chunky — like a physical clay toy. No text, no wordmark, no lettering anywhere.

**SCENE:**
Pure white studio background, completely clean and featureless. The globe floats at the center of the frame. A single soft key light positioned at the upper-left at roughly 45 degrees creates a bright creamy highlight across the upper-left quadrant of the sphere and a gentle shadow gradient toward the lower-right. A very subtle warm amber-brown elliptical shadow sits directly beneath the globe, slightly blurred, as if the object is resting a few millimeters above a frosted white surface. No environment, no props, no gradient background — complete isolation for logo use.

**STYLE:**
3D clay product render — not photorealistic, not flat illustration. The aesthetic sits between a Pixar short film prop and a premium mobile game app icon. Soft clean clay surface with gentle subsurface scattering in the amber body to sell the clay material. Crisp clean silhouette edge. No depth-of-field blur — the entire subject is sharp. No film grain, no texture overlay. 1:1 square aspect ratio. Ultra-high resolution suitable for a 1024×1024 app icon. Ambient occlusion in the recessed areas where the fork and spoon sit against the sphere. Gentle specular highlight on the upper-left of the sphere — small, soft, not blown out.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT:**
flat illustration, 2D cartoon, photorealistic rendering, harsh black shadows, grey shadows, neon colors, text overlays, watermark, logo text, wordmark, geographic accuracy on continents, sharp edges anywhere on the object, overblown highlights, lens flare, background gradient, blurred subject, depth of field, film grain, noise, low resolution, digital artifacts, fish-eye distortion, stretched proportions, asymmetric fork tines, extra utensils, extra objects in frame, busy background.

## GENERATION 3 — Bito Expression Sheet (4 Poses)

> Goal: Four Bito expressions on a single image — a character model sheet for the illustrator and animator. Paste this prompt exactly as written.

---

**CHARACTER:**
A clean 2×2 grid reference sheet showing four versions of the same clay character Bito (small round globe-bodied clay mascot: amber sphere body with teal continent patches, tiny tilted white chef's hat, stubby clay arms and legs, big expressive eyes). Each quadrant shows one distinct emotional state. All four versions are identical in scale, same clay art style, same lighting treatment.

TOP-LEFT — HAPPY / DEFAULT: Bito smiling warmly, both arms relaxed at sides with palms slightly open and facing outward, standing in a relaxed confident pose. This is the everyday guide expression — approachable, friendly, calm.

TOP-RIGHT — EXCITED / CELEBRATING: Bito with a huge open-mouth grin showing small rounded teeth, both arms thrown up in a wide V-shape victory pose, a tiny burst of small sparkle stars and confetti dots around the chef's hat. The eyes are wide and lit up. Used for recipe unlock celebration moments.

BOTTOM-LEFT — CURIOUS / THINKING: Bito with the right arm raised and the index finger gently touching the cheek below one eye (thinking pose). Eyebrows — suggested by slightly raised clay ridge shapes above the eyes — arched upward. A small thought bubble (single cloud shape) hovers to one side. Mild head tilt. Used when giving food facts and country information.

BOTTOM-RIGHT — WAVING / GREETING: Bito with the right arm raised high in a clear enthusiastic wave, left arm on hip, biggest warmest smile of the four. The chef's hat slightly displaced from the wave energy. Used on onboarding screens and when greeting returning players.

Below each of the four quadrants: a small simple text label in a clean rounded sans-serif — "Happy", "Excited!", "Curious", "Hello!" — in dark chocolate brown on a light background chip. The labels are part of the reference sheet, not floating text — they sit in a small cream-coloured label pill below each character.

**SCENE:**
Pure clean white background across the entire sheet. Perfectly even flat studio lighting — no directional shadows, no key-light drama — so all four quadrants read with identical visual weight. Each of the four Bito variants has a very subtle warm drop shadow sitting directly below their feet, radius-blurred, barely visible — just enough to ground them on the surface. Generous white space between the four quadrants as gutters. The overall document feels like a professional character design handoff sheet from a studio — clean, structured, clearly readable.

**STYLE:**
Professional character model sheet / expression reference sheet. 3D clay render aesthetic — soft, toy-like clay material consistent across all four quadrants. Same lighting, same material, same level of clay depth in all four. 1:1 square aspect ratio — the 2×2 grid fills the square. High resolution at minimum 2048×2048 — this is a reference document. No film grain, no background texture, no depth of field, no vignette. Clean silhouette on all four characters. Even white space as gutters between quadrants. Consistent character scale across all four — same height, same proportions, only the pose and expression changes. Professional game character asset quality.

**NEGATIVE PROMPT:**
inconsistent character design between quadrants, different proportions per quadrant, different art styles per quadrant, directional shadows, dramatic lighting, dark background, gradient background, busy background, text overlays beyond the four approved labels, watermark, low resolution, blurred edges, depth of field, film grain, noise, extra characters, extra limbs, sharp edges, scary expressions, inconsistent color between quadrants, photorealistic rendering, flat 2D illustration, cel shading, digital artifacts, misaligned grid, unequal quadrant sizes.

## Tips for best results in Gemini

- Use **Gemini 2.0 Flash** or **Imagen 4** for these — the clay 3D style renders best on the latest models.
- Generate at least **4 variations per prompt** and pick the best one — especially for the character, consistency between generations matters.
- For the Expression Sheet (Generation 3): if Gemini struggles with the 2×2 layout, try asking for just one expression at a time using Generation 2's prompt with the expression description swapped in.
- Once you have a Bito generation you love — save it and use it as a **reference image** (image-to-image or style-lock) for subsequent generations to keep him consistent.
- The logo (Generation 1) should look close to a polished app icon. If it comes out too illustrative or too photorealistic, add "clay toy render, soft material, not glossy" to the style block.
